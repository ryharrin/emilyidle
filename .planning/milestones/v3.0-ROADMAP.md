## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**
- Catalog cards are the primary purchase flow (Vault embeds catalog shopping).
- Cash remains career-salary driven; watch ownership is not a cash faucet.
- Watch ownership is model-based with explicit duplicates diminishing returns.
- Events multiply both enjoyment and dollars; displayed rates match sim accrual.
- Upgrades are reframed to affect enjoyment progression (not dollars), keeping previews truthful.

**Issues Resolved:**
- Consolidated catalog-first purchase surface and removed split watch-buy UI.
- Added model-based purchasing, wear-one bonus, interactions mini-games.
- Closed v3.0 milestone audit gaps around rate previews and event/cash consistency.

**Issues Deferred:**
- Interactions and cooldowns are tier-based (WatchItemId) rather than per-model.
- Rates help copy still contains some legacy “vault dollars/sec” phrasing.
