<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Style actionable vs non-actionable cards in CSS</name>
  <files>src/style.css, tests/catalog-actionable-visual.spec.ts</files>
  <action>
  - Add CSS rules near the existing `.catalog-card` section to make `catalog-actionable` stand out clearly and `catalog-nonactionable` read as muted.
  - Prefer stable, testable computed-style deltas:
    - Ensure base `.catalog-card` does NOT have a box-shadow.
    - Ensure `.catalog-card.catalog-actionable` has a non-`none` `box-shadow` (e.g. a subtle glow/outline) and `opacity: 1`.
    - Ensure `.catalog-card.catalog-nonactionable` has `box-shadow: none` and an `opacity` < 1 (e.g. `0.75`).
  - Ensure the styling works in both dark (default) and `[data-theme="light"]` modes.
  - Avoid changing layout metrics that could cause major reflow; prefer border/color/shadow/opacity.
  - Ensure the `purchase-flash` animation still reads and is not visually swallowed by the new states.
  - Add a Playwright test that proves the visual distinction (computed style, not just class presence):
    - Create `tests/catalog-actionable-visual.spec.ts`.
    - Seed localStorage via `page.addInitScript` with a save payload similar to `tests/catalog-images.spec.ts`, but with enough `currencyCents` that at least one `data-testid^="catalog-buy-"` button renders.
    - Navigate to `/`, click the "Catalog" tab, and wait for `data-testid="catalog-grid"`.
    - Find an actionable card by locating `[data-testid^="catalog-buy-"]` and taking its closest `article[data-testid="catalog-card"]`.
    - Find a non-actionable card by locating `[data-testid^="catalog-gate-"]` and taking its closest `article[data-testid="catalog-card"]`.
    - Assert computed styles differ in a stable way:
      - actionable opacity === "1" and box-shadow != "none"
      - non-actionable opacity < "1" and box-shadow === "none"
    - Repeat the same assertions after switching to light theme by updating `emily-idle:settings` (`themeMode: "light"`) and reloading.
  </action>
  <verify>pnpm run test:e2e -- tests/catalog-actionable-visual.spec.ts</verify>
  <done>
  - Actionable cards are visually distinct at a glance from non-actionable cards in both themes.
  </done>
</task>
