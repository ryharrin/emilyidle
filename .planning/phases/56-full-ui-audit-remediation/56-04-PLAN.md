<objective>
Reduce Catalog friction by simplifying card controls, clarifying blocked states, and strengthening buy-path readability.
</objective>

<tasks>
- Limit default card controls to a dominant buy action plus one secondary action; move tertiary actions into details.
- Condense gated purchase messaging to concise taxonomy labels with direct next-step guidance.
- Add "next affordable" signal (time or requirement) in card metadata to guide short-horizon decisions.
- Improve mobile card spacing and sticky affordances to avoid repetitive tap/scroll loops.
</tasks>

<acceptance_criteria>
1. Default card face never presents more than two direct action buttons before expansion.
2. Every disabled buy state includes one concise reason and one actionable next step.
3. Affordable watch options are discoverable in the first two mobile screen heights for seeded states.
4. Catalog CTA behavior remains deterministic across desktop and mobile projects.
</acceptance_criteria>
