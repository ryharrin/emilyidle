# Phase 35: Balance & Help Clarity - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-01-30
**Status:** In discussion (design updates pending)

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

This phase adjusts pacing and early-game career onboarding, then updates help/copy to match.

In scope:
- Make early career progression slower (level curve pacing).
- Rework early economy so no cash accrues automatically on a fresh save.
- Add an explicit early Career action to start earning money ("enter PhD program" -> small stipend).
- Resolve naming overlap: "Private practice" should not be both a career path and a career stage.
- Career UI ordering and visibility rules:
  - Sessions card appears at the top of the Career page.
  - Track/stage choice options are not clickable until the stated unlock level.
  - Only the *next* stage choice surface is visible (avoid showing multiple future choice grids at once).

Out of scope:
- Quartz mini-game alignment (Phase 36).

</domain>
