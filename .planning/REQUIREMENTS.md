# Requirements: Emily Idle

**Defined:** 2026-01-30
**Core Value:** A satisfying watch-collecting idle loop that saves reliably and stays pleasant to play and maintain.

## v3.1 Requirements: Career Depth & Landing

Requirements for this milestone (v3.1 Career Depth & Landing).

### Career Landing & Navigation

- [x] **CAREER-LAND-01**: Fresh saves land on Career by default (deep links override; existing saves keep predictable last-tab behavior).
- [x] **CAREER-LAND-02**: Career tab is reachable immediately and is the primary early-game surface (stable UI anchors; no selector churn).

### Career Stages & Permanent Choices

- [x] **CAREER-STAGE-01**: Career progression has at least 5 stages, starting at grad student and ending at private practice.
- [x] **CAREER-STAGE-02**: Each stage after the first presents at least one permanent choice that meaningfully changes money-earning potential and/or cadence (without introducing non-career cash faucets).
- [x] **CAREER-STAGE-03**: Choices have clear before/after previews and are persisted in saves.

### Progress Feedback & Next Actions

- [x] **CAREER-PROG-01**: Career page shows progress toward the next unlock (progress bar + explicit “next unlock” callout).
- [x] **CAREER-PROG-02**: Career page shows a “next action” cue that nudges the player toward the fastest way to progress.

### Balance & Clarity

- [ ] **CAREER-BAL-01**: Career pacing feels smooth across stages (no deadlocks; stage unlocks are achievable without grind spikes).
- [x] **CAREER-CLAR-01**: Help/copy explains the career progression loop, stages, and choices clearly.

### Bug Fixes (Carry-Forward)

- [x] **BUG-UI-01**: Quartz set-time mini-game watch display is aligned properly on mobile and desktop.
